## Original industrial and toy 3D models

The STEP, STL, OBJ, GLB, glTF, PLY, 3MF and VTK demo entries use eight different
project-authored models: a bearing assembly, impeller, toy rover, space robot,
industrial arm, fox figurine, planetary gearbox and cutaway heat exchanger.
All are self-contained original geometry under the project MIT license, with
no third-party meshes, textures, brands or licensed characters. Details,
generation commands and format features are in [models/SOURCES.md](models/SOURCES.md).
Their thumbnails and the homepage model card are captures of the real previewer.

## Original archive specimens

The archive catalog includes 38 project-authored specimens: extension packages,
ZIPX and comic containers, compressed streams/TARs, AR/DEB/CPIO, Microsoft Cabinet
and ISO 9660/Joliet/UDF images, LHA/LZH, RPM/XAR and WIM/ESD images. The field-notes
collection adds original seasonal SVG artwork and a JSON palette. All packaged text and artwork is MIT-licensed original
Filens material. Real container/codec details, regeneration commands and preview
limits are documented in [archives/README.md](archives/README.md).

## Original text files

Text offers 42 project-authored original files across 40 format identifiers:
notes, Markdown/reStructuredText/AsciiDoc/LaTeX documents, eighteen code examples,
fifteen configuration files and two logs. They replace the previous nine short
inline fixtures and add thirty-three examples. All are MIT-licensed fictional
studies, loaded as original UTF-8 files through the native text previewer.
The catalog includes Chinese and multilingual content, Makefile tabs and a CRLF
log with a multiline diagnostic trace. Full inventory and encoding details:
[text/SOURCES.md](text/SOURCES.md).

## Native design files

Design files offers twelve project-authored original files: six PSD, two PSB, two
PDF-compatible AI documents, one EPS and one two-page PS document. Native PSD/PSB previews decode saved composite pixels
in the shared WASM core; compatible AI artwork preserves vector paths and type
through the existing PDF viewer. Uploaded files use the same path. WebP images
are picker thumbnails only. EPS/PS and legacy PostScript AI are interpreted in
the on-demand WASM module to vector PDF, with explicit errors for unsupported
artwork or missing fonts. Resident font outlines are excluded from this compact build.
Layouts and illustrations are project-authored; three Unsplash photographs and
the AI documents' OFL font subsets retain their separate licenses. Full sources,
encoding limits and rights distinctions are in [design/SOURCES.md](design/SOURCES.md).
Regenerate with `node scripts/generate-photoshop-samples.mjs`.

External proprietary-format validation files are not redistributed with the demo:

The demo loads these originals on demand through public `/external-samples/` proxies. Vite development/preview, `scripts/serve-static.py`, and `vercel.json` provide the routes. They do not use NAS storage or authentication.

- Insta360 One RS 1-inch `.INSP`, `.INSV`, and `.LRV` samples: https://360rumors.com/download-1-inch-360/ — the source permits personal viewing only. Browser validation uses the 6528x3264 JPEG-based `.INSP`, the 768x384 H.264 `.INSV` proxy, and the 768x384 H.264 low-resolution `.LRV` dual-fisheye proxy (`LRV_20220625_140410_11_008.insv`) from the associated Google Drive folder.
- DJI Osmo 360 `.OSV` and matching `.LRF` samples: https://huggingface.co/datasets/SamuelLDX/osmo360-lidar-preview — the dataset license is marked `other`. Browser validation uses the camera-generated 2048x1024 H.264 `.LRF` proxy rather than attempting to decode the dual-fisheye HEVC streams directly.
